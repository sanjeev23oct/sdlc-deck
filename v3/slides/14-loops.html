Loops

Done means checked, with evidence

1 · MakerBuilds one small task
2 · CheckerTests it against the gates
3 · EvidenceWrites the result to a file
4 · Tick or repairPass ticks the task. Failure goes back to the maker.

The loop stops when the task passes, a limit is reached or a person needs to decide.

ControlOnly a checker that wrote evidence can tick a task.
